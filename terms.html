1. The service

CC-GEN generates mathematically valid but financially empty credit card numbers that pass the Luhn checksum algorithm. They are provided exclusively for software testing: payment gateway sandboxes, QA pipelines, form validation, and UI design.

2. Acceptable use

  • Use generated numbers only against test/sandbox environments or your own systems.
  • Do not attempt purchases, account registrations, or any transaction on real payment networks with generated numbers.
  • Do not use CC-GEN to misrepresent identity, commit fraud, or violate any applicable law.

3. Sandbox testing policy

Every number produced by CC-GEN possesses zero financial balance and is rejected by real merchant processors. Real payment networks, banks, and processors are not affiliated with CC-GEN, and network names (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) appear only to indicate the number ranges being imitated. If you are unsure whether an environment is a true sandbox, do not use it.
