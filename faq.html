In your sandbox environment (e.g. Stripe Test Mode with pk_test_... keys), input any of our standard test card numbers. You can test various checkout scenarios including:

  • Successful payment authorization (e.g. Visa 4242 4242 4242 4242)
  • 3D Secure (3DS) authentication challenges
  • Card declines and invalid expiration scenarios

You can cancel or manage your subscription anytime without penalties:

  1. Sign in to your account from the top right menu.
  2. Navigate to Account Settings → Billing & Plans.
  3. Click Cancel Subscription or select a new tier.

Your subscription features will remain active until the end of your current billing period.

To reset your password, visit the Sign In page and click "Forgot Password?". Enter your registered email address to receive a secure password recovery link. If you are already logged in, you can change your password under Settings → Security.

We do not store or transmit sensitive financial credentials because CC-GEN only generates algorithmic dummy placeholders. All client communication is encrypted using TLS 1.3, and any mock project datasets you save to your workspace are isolated and protected by strict role-based access control.

Deleted projects and card datasets are temporarily moved to your account's Trash / Archive folder for 30 days before permanent deletion. You can restore any deleted dataset during this window with a single click from your user dashboard.

CC-GEN supports bulk exports in JSON, CSV, XML, and SQL Insert scripts. These can be imported directly into test fixtures, seed databases, or automated UI testing pipelines like Selenium, Playwright, or Postman.
